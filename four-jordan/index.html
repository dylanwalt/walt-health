Jeff Nippard workout

Same 3-day shape as Plan C — push, pull, shoulders — every cue below is from Jeff Nippard, with links to the video or site.

“Tension is universally recognized as the primary factor driving muscle growth.”
Jeff Nippard · How To Train For Pure Muscle Growth
~75–90 min Last set → failure Rest ≥ 1 min

Push · chest + shoulders

Chest, front/side delts, triceps. Control the negative. Progress load or reps next week.

1

Incline press (DB or barbell)

3 × 8–10 Rest 90–180s 1–2 RIR early
Jeff says “Bring the dumbbells all the way down on presses until you feel a nice deep stretch in your pecs.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Push workout
2

Shoulder press (seated DB / machine)

3 × 8–10 Rest 90–180s Leave 1–2 in tank
Jeff says “I pretty much always leave a rep or two in the tank on heavy free weight compound exercises… even on the last set.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Shoulder exercises
3

Side lateral raise (cable preferred)

4 × 12–15 Rest 60–90s Last set → failure
Jeff says “Raise the cable height… tension is highest whenever the cable and your arm form a 90° angle.” Stretch across the body — don’t stop at your side.
Watch · Lateral raise fix Best shoulder moves
4

Chest fly (cable / machine)

3 × 12–15 Rest 60–90s Last set hard
Jeff says “Machine and cable-based exercises… provide massive tension for less fatigue than free weights and also tend to be safer to take to failure.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth
5

Triceps pushdown

3 × 10–15 Rest 60–90s Last set → failure
Jeff says “Do two to three sets on most exercises and take the last set all the way to failure… leave a few reps in the tank for the earlier sets and send it to Hell on the last set.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Failure short
6

Leg press or goblet squat

2 × 10–12 Rest 90–180s Maintenance
Jeff says Control the negative near failure — “as you do those last few reps you still control the negative.”
Watch · Training to failure
7

Easy cardio finisher

~10 min Zone 2 After lifting
Jeff says Do cardio after lifting (or separate) so it doesn’t steal strength from the session.
Watch · Cardio without losing muscle

Pull · back + arms

Lats, upper back, rear delts, biceps. Full stretch. Last isolation set to failure.

1

Lat pulldown or assisted pull-up

4 × 8–10 Rest 90–180s Full stretch
Jeff says “On pull-ups you go all the way up and all the way down” — get a deep stretch at the top of the pulldown.
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Pull workout
2

Seated cable row

4 × 8–10 Rest 90–180s Progress load
Jeff says “You need to apply the progressive overload principle where you aim to increase some training variable from week to week.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth
3

Rear delt fly

3 × 12–15 Rest 60–90s Last set hard
Jeff says “We generally want a controlled 2 to 4 second negative” — resist the weight on the way down.
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Shoulder exercises
4

Face pull

3 × 12–15 Rest 60–90s
Jeff says Cables give “smooth even tension throughout the range of motion” — safer to push hard than swinging free weights.
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Pull workout
5

Biceps curl (DB / EZ / cable)

3 × 10–12 Rest 60–90s Last set → failure
Jeff says Don’t swing — “if I curl the weight up while leaning back… I’m shifting tension away from the biceps.” Keep momentum under control.
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth
6

Romanian deadlift or leg curl

3 × 8–12 Rest 90–180s 1–2 RIR
Jeff says Leave a rep or two on fatiguing compounds — failure isn’t worth the recovery cost on every heavy hinge.
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Failure short
7

Easy cardio finisher

~10 min Incline walk
Jeff says Cardio after the lift — keep it easy so it supports the plan, not wrecks recovery.
Watch · Cardio short

Shoulders · priority day

Side delts get the volume. Same menu next week — beat the log.

1

Shoulder press

4 × 6–10 Rest 90–180s 1–2 RIR
Jeff says “Leave a few reps in the tank for the earlier sets and send it to Hell on the last set.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Shoulder exercises
2

Side lateral raise

5 × 12–15 Rest 60–90s Main builder
Jeff says “Stretch the cable across your body instead of stopping at your side.” Cable laterals load the side delt in the stretch.
Watch · Lateral raise fix
3

Incline press

3 × 10–12 Rest 90–180s
Jeff says “Tension is King… the force that our muscles experience when they stretch and contract under load.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth
4

Side laterals · pump set

3 × 12–20 Rest 60–90s Last set → failure
Jeff says On laterals: “resisting the weight on the way down and then driving the dumbbells up and out forcefully on the positive.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Lateral raise fix
5

Triceps pushdown

3 × 10–12 Rest 60–90s Last set → failure
Jeff says “If you never take any sets to failure, how are you supposed to know what one to two reps in the tank feels like?”
Watch · Failure short
6

Leg press / lunges

2 × 10–12 Rest 90–180s
Jeff says “Resting at least 1 minute between sets is generally better than resting for less than 1 minute.”
Watch · Pure Muscle Growth Rest periods
7

Cable crunch / knee raise

3 × 10–15 Rest 60–90s
Jeff says Train hard, then recover — abs work builds the muscle; fat loss still comes from the whole plan (lift + protein + consistency).
jeffnippard.com Pure Muscle Growth
8

Easy cardio finisher

~10 min Zone 2
Jeff says Cardio after weights — don’t turn it into a HIIT wreck before you’ve earned the lift.
Watch · Cardio short
